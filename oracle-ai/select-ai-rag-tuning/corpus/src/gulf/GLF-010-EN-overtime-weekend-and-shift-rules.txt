## 1. Purpose and scope
This policy sets out the working week, the weekly rest days, overtime pay and the rules for shift work at Meridian Systems Gulf FZ-LLC. It applies to employees at the head office in Dubai (United Arab Emirates), the Riyadh branch (Kingdom of Saudi Arabia) and the Doha branch (State of Qatar).

This is a company policy. Where the labour law of the country of employment gives an employee a more favourable entitlement than this policy, the more favourable entitlement applies and payroll is adjusted accordingly.

## 2. Working week and weekly rest days
| Location | Working days | Weekly rest days | Standard hours | Office hours |
| Dubai, UAE | Monday to Friday | Saturday and Sunday | 8 hours a day, 40 hours a week | 09:00 to 18:00 |
| Riyadh, KSA | Sunday to Thursday | Friday and Saturday | 8 hours a day, 40 hours a week | 08:00 to 17:00 |
| Doha, Qatar | Sunday to Thursday | Friday and Saturday | 8 hours a day, 40 hours a week | 07:30 to 16:30 |

Office hours include an unpaid meal break of 60 minutes. In Dubai, the Friday break is extended to **90 minutes**, from 12:15 to 13:45, to allow time for Friday prayer. The additional 30 minutes is made up flexibly within the same week.

During the holy month of Ramadan, the working day at all three locations is reduced to **6 hours**, or 30 hours a week, with no reduction in salary. Each location head publishes the Ramadan timings at least 7 days before the month begins.

## 3. Who is eligible for overtime
- Paid overtime applies to employees in grades **G1 to G4**.
- Employees in **G5 and above** do not receive overtime pay. When asked to work on a weekly rest day or a public holiday, they earn **Time Off in Lieu (TOIL)**: one day for a full day worked, or half a day for 4 hours or more. TOIL must be taken within **90 days** of being earned and is never paid in cash.
- Overtime must be approved in MyMeridian by the reporting manager before it is worked. Retrospective approval is allowed only for a declared production incident, and only by the function head.
- During Ramadan, hours worked beyond the 6-hour Ramadan working day count as overtime.

## 4. Overtime rates
Overtime is calculated on the basic hourly rate: monthly basic salary divided by 30, then divided by 8. Housing, transport and other allowances are excluded.

| Overtime type | Dubai, UAE | Riyadh, KSA | Doha, Qatar |
| Working day, before 22:00 | 125% | 150% | 125% |
| Working day, 22:00 to 05:00 | 150% | 175% | 150% |
| Weekly rest day | 175% | 200% | 150% plus one replacement rest day |
| Public holiday | 200% plus one replacement day off | 250% | 200% plus one replacement day off |

Worked example: a G3 engineer in Dubai with a monthly basic salary of AED 9,000 has a basic hourly rate of AED 37.50. Four hours of approved overtime on a working day before 22:00 are paid at 125%, giving a total of **AED 187.50**.

## 5. Overtime limits and payment
| Limit | Dubai, UAE | Riyadh, KSA | Doha, Qatar |
| Maximum overtime per working day | 2 hours | 2 hours | 2 hours |
| Maximum overtime per calendar month | 30 hours | 36 hours | 32 hours |
| Maximum weekly rest days worked per month | 2 | 2 | 1 |

- The monthly limit may be exceeded only for a declared production incident, with the written approval of both the function head and the Head of HR Gulf.
- Approved overtime is paid with the following month's salary and appears as a separate earning line on the payslip.
- Overtime entries not approved by the 25th of the month roll into the next payroll cycle.

## 6. Shift work
Shift rules apply to employees rostered in the 24x7 Service Operations Centre and to field support teams.

- Shift rosters are published in MyMeridian at least **14 days** in advance.
- A shift lasts 9 hours, including a 60-minute meal break.
- There must be at least **11 hours** of rest between the end of one shift and the start of the next.
- No employee works more than **6 consecutive night shifts**. After a run of night shifts, the employee receives at least 48 hours off.
- Shift swaps are requested in MyMeridian at least 48 hours in advance and approved by the shift lead.
- A rostered shift that falls on a weekly rest day is not overtime. The roster must give the employee two rest days elsewhere in the same week.

| Shift allowance, per shift | Dubai, UAE | Riyadh, KSA | Doha, Qatar |
| Night shift starting at 20:00 or later | AED 50 | SAR 55 | QAR 50 |
| Rostered shift on a public holiday | AED 120 | SAR 130 | QAR 120 |

## 7. Health, safety and fatigue
- From **01 June to 15 September**, field engineers must not carry out outdoor work at client sites between **12:30 and 15:30** at any of the three locations. Managers plan site visits around this pause.
- An employee called out between 00:00 and 05:00 for more than 2 hours may start **late** on the next working day by an equal number of hours, without any deduction from leave.
- Managers review team overtime every month. An employee who reaches the monthly limit in 2 consecutive months is referred to the HR Business Partner for a workload review.
