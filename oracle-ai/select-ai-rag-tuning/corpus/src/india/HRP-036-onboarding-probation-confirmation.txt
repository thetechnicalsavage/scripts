## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out what happens from the acceptance of an offer through to confirmation in the role, and what both the employee and the manager are accountable for.

## 2. Before the joining date
The candidate completes pre-joining formalities in the onboarding portal at least 3 working days before the joining date:

- Personal details, address, emergency contact.
- PAN, Aadhaar, and the Universal Account Number if one exists.
- Bank account details with a cancelled cheque or a bank statement header.
- Educational certificates, the relieving letter and the last three payslips from the previous employer.
- Passport-size photograph.
- Signed offer acceptance, confidentiality undertaking and code of conduct acknowledgement.
- Provident fund and gratuity nominations.
- Form 12B from the previous employer, where the candidate is joining mid financial year.

The HR Business Partner confirms the workstation, laptop, access card and system accounts with Workplace Services and IT **5 working days before** the joining date, so that the employee is productive on day one.

## 3. Day one and the first week
| Day | Activity |
| Day 1 | Welcome and documentation, access card, laptop handover, workstation allocation, introduction to the team |
| Day 1 | Mandatory induction: Code of Conduct, POSH, information security, data privacy, health and safety |
| Day 2 | Role induction with the reporting manager, introduction to the buddy |
| Day 3 to 5 | Systems access verification, tool familiarisation, engagement or project induction |
| End of week 1 | The 30-60-90 day plan agreed and recorded in MyMeridian |

Every new joiner is assigned a **buddy**: a peer, not the manager, who answers the questions a new joiner may not want to take to a manager. The buddy relationship runs for 90 days.

## 4. The 30-60-90 day plan
The manager and the employee agree written objectives for the first three months, recorded in MyMeridian:
- **By day 30**: understand the team, the client, the systems and the ways of working; complete all mandatory training; deliver a first small piece of work end to end.
- **By day 60**: work independently on defined tasks; contribute in reviews; know who to approach for what.
- **By day 90**: deliver to the standard expected of the grade; own a defined area.

Progress is reviewed at each milestone. **A concern at day 30 must be raised at day 30**, not saved for the confirmation review. An employee cannot correct a problem they have not been told about.

## 5. Probation
| Grade band | Probation period |
| G1 to G4 | 6 months |
| G5 and above | 3 months |
| Re-hire within 12 months of the previous separation | 3 months |

During probation:
- Casual Leave and Sick Leave are available from day one. Earned Leave accrues but can be availed only after confirmation, per HRP-001.
- The notice period is **15 days** on either side, per HRP-040.
- The employee is not eligible for a salary advance under HRP-009, for stock options under HRP-014, or to apply internally under HRP-035.
- Group medical, accident and life insurance cover applies from day one.
- Provident fund and gratuity service count from the date of joining.

## 6. Extension of probation
Probation may be extended **once**, by up to 3 months, where performance is short of the standard but the manager judges it recoverable. An extension requires:
- A written review setting out the specific gaps with evidence.
- Agreed, measurable objectives for the extension period.
- The HR Business Partner's approval.
- Communication to the employee in writing **before the original probation end date**.

An extension communicated after the probation period has ended is not valid.

## 7. Confirmation
Confirmation is **not automatic**. The manager completes the confirmation review in MyMeridian **15 days before** the probation ends, recording the assessment against the 30-60-90 plan and a recommendation: confirm, extend or separate.

- Where the manager takes no action and the probation period ends, the HR Business Partner escalates to the function head. The employee is not disadvantaged by the manager's delay: if no decision is recorded within 15 days of the probation end date, the employee is confirmed with effect from the original date.
- On confirmation the employee receives a confirmation letter, becomes eligible for the full leave, benefit and internal mobility entitlements, and moves to the standard notice period.

## 8. Separation during probation
Where performance or conduct during probation does not meet the standard, employment may be ended with **15 days notice** or pay in lieu. Before that decision:
- The concerns must have been raised with the employee in writing at least once, with a reasonable opportunity to improve.
- The HR Business Partner must review the case.
- The reason is recorded. An employee separated during probation for performance is eligible for rehire; the record reflects that.

Separation during probation for **misconduct** follows HRP-028, not this section.
