## 1. What this guide covers
Where to find your payslip, how to open it, how to make your tax declarations, when Form 16 is available, and how to get a salary certificate. The underlying rules are in HRP-010 and HRP-016.

## 2. Payslips
- Location: **MyMeridian > My Pay > Payslips**.
- The payslip for a month is published on the **first working day of the following month**. Salary itself is credited on the **last working day** of the month.
- Payslips are available for the last 36 months in the portal. For anything older, raise an AskHR ticket.
- Download as PDF individually, or select multiple months and download as a single archive, which is what most banks and consulates ask for.

### Opening the password-protected PDF
The password is the **first four characters of your PAN in upper case, followed by your date of birth in DDMM format**.

For example, an employee with PAN ABCDE1234F and a date of birth of 09 March 1990 uses the password **ABCD0903**.

If the password does not work, the usual cause is a date of birth recorded incorrectly. Check MyMeridian > My Profile and raise an AskHR ticket to correct it.

## 3. Reading the payslip
| Section | What it contains |
| Earnings | Basic, House Rent Allowance, Flexible Benefit Plan components, shift allowance, on-call allowance, overtime, arrears |
| Deductions | Provident Fund, Professional Tax, income tax deducted at source, salary advance recovery, any other recovery |
| Employer contributions | Employer Provident Fund, gratuity provision, insurance premium. **These are part of cost to company, not deductions from your pay** |
| Leave summary | Opening, accrued, availed and closing balance for each leave type |
| Year to date | Cumulative earnings, deductions and tax for the financial year |

## 4. Choosing your tax regime
- Window: **01 to 20 April** each year, in MyMeridian > My Pay > Tax Regime.
- The **new regime under section 115BAC is the statutory default**. If you make no selection, the new regime applies.
- The selection **cannot be changed during the financial year** in payroll. You may still choose differently when you file your income tax return, subject to the rules that apply to you.
- The portal shows a side-by-side comparison of the estimated annual tax under both regimes, based on your current salary and your declared investments. Use it before choosing.

## 5. Investment declaration and proof submission
| Stage | Window | What happens |
| Declaration | 01 to 20 April | You declare what you **intend** to invest. Payroll spreads the tax benefit across 12 months |
| Revision | Any time up to 30 November | You can revise the declared amounts as your plans change |
| Proof submission | 01 December to 15 January | You upload evidence of what you **actually** invested |
| Recomputation | February payroll | Any exemption without proof is withdrawn and the shortfall is recovered across February and March |

The **15 January deadline is firm**. Payroll must have the final position to compute the year's tax across two remaining payrolls. If you obtain a proof after the deadline, you can still claim the deduction directly in your income tax return and receive the refund from the tax department.

### Uploading proofs
Go to **MyMeridian > My Pay > Tax Declarations > Upload Proofs**. Each declared item has its own upload slot. Files must be PDF or JPG and under 5 MB. The portal shows the status of each item: Not submitted, Under review, Accepted, or Rejected with a reason.

Common rejection reasons: a receipt in someone else's name; a premium receipt for a policy that lapsed; rent receipts without the landlord's PAN where annual rent exceeds INR 1,00,000; a provisional housing loan certificate not naming the employee as a borrower.

## 6. Form 12BB and previous employer income
- **Form 12BB** is generated automatically from your declarations and must be reviewed and submitted by 15 January.
- If you joined Meridian part way through the financial year, upload **Form 12B** and the previous employer's payslips and tax deduction details in MyMeridian > My Pay > Previous Employer Income, by 30 November. If you do not, both employers give you the basic exemption separately and you may face a significant liability when you file your return.

## 7. Form 16
- Available by **15 June** for the financial year that ended on 31 March.
- Location: **MyMeridian > My Pay > Tax Documents**.
- **Part A** is generated by the income tax department's TRACES system and shows the tax deducted and deposited against your PAN. **Part B** is generated by payroll and shows the salary breakup and the deductions allowed.
- The same password rule as the payslip applies.
- A correction request must be raised within 30 days of issue through an AskHR ticket in the Payroll category. Corrections to Part A require a revised return to be filed with the tax department and take up to 45 days.

## 8. Salary certificates and bank letters
Raise an AskHR ticket in the Payroll category for:
- A salary certificate for a loan application.
- An employment verification letter for a visa or a landlord.
- A compensation confirmation for a background check by another employer.

These are issued within **3 working days**. Specify the addressee and any format the recipient requires.
