## 1. Purpose
This document sets out the statutory social security benefits that apply to employees of Meridian Systems India Private Limited, the contribution rates, and the process for claiming each benefit.

## 2. Employees' Provident Fund
| Item | Provision |
| Governing law | Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act 1952 |
| Employee contribution | 12 per cent of Basic plus Dearness Allowance |
| Employer contribution | 12 per cent, split as 8.33 per cent to the Employees' Pension Scheme and 3.67 per cent to the Provident Fund |
| Statutory wage ceiling | INR 15,000 per month for the purpose of the mandatory contribution |
| Meridian practice | Contribution is computed on actual Basic plus Dearness Allowance, not restricted to the ceiling, unless the employee opts for the ceiling at the time of joining |
| Interest rate | 8.25 per cent declared for financial year 2024-25 |
| Administrative charges | Borne entirely by Meridian |

Every employee has a **Universal Account Number (UAN)**, which stays the same across employers. On joining, the employee declares the existing UAN in MyMeridian so that the previous balance can be transferred. Where the employee has no UAN, Meridian generates one within 30 days of joining.

### Voluntary Provident Fund
An employee may contribute more than 12 per cent as Voluntary Provident Fund, up to 100 per cent of Basic plus Dearness Allowance. The employer contribution does not increase. The election is made during the Flexible Benefit Plan window in April and can be changed once in October. Note that interest on the employee's own contribution above INR 2,50,000 in a financial year is taxable.

### Withdrawal and advances
Partial withdrawal is permitted from the EPFO portal directly by the employee for house purchase, marriage, education, medical treatment and other purposes prescribed in the scheme. Meridian's role is limited to attesting the claim where the employer attestation is required. Raise an AskHR ticket in the Payroll category with the claim form; attestation is completed within 5 working days.

## 3. Employees' State Insurance
| Item | Provision |
| Governing law | Employees' State Insurance Act 1948 |
| Applicability | Employees with gross wages up to INR 21,000 per month |
| Employee contribution | 0.75 per cent of gross wages |
| Employer contribution | 3.25 per cent of gross wages |
| Benefits | Medical care at ESIC facilities, sickness benefit, maternity benefit, disablement benefit, dependants benefit, funeral expenses |

An employee covered by ESI is not simultaneously covered by the Meridian group medical policy for the same benefit, but Meridian extends the group personal accident and group term life cover to all employees regardless of ESI coverage.

## 4. Gratuity
| Item | Provision |
| Governing law | Payment of Gratuity Act 1972 |
| Eligibility | 5 years of continuous service, waived in the event of death or disablement |
| Rate | 15 days of wages for each completed year of service |
| Formula | (Last drawn Basic + Dearness Allowance) x 15 x completed years of service, divided by 26 |
| Treatment of part years | A part year of 6 months or more counts as a full year |
| Statutory ceiling | INR 20,00,000 |
| Tax treatment | Exempt under section 10(10) up to the statutory ceiling |
| Payment timeline | Within 30 days of the last working day |

### Worked example
An employee with a last drawn Basic plus Dearness Allowance of INR 80,000 per month and 8 years and 7 months of service:
Completed years = 9, because 7 months is more than 6 months.
Gratuity = (80,000 x 15 x 9) / 26 = **INR 4,15,385**.

### Nomination
Every employee must file a gratuity nomination in **Form F** within 30 days of joining, and update it on marriage or on the birth of a child. The nomination is filed in MyMeridian > My Profile > Nominations. An unfiled nomination delays payment to the family in the event of death, because the company must then satisfy itself about the legal heirs.

## 5. Professional Tax
Professional Tax is a state levy deducted monthly from salary and paid to the state government. The rate depends on the state of the work location and on the salary slab. It is shown as a deduction in the payslip and is allowed as a deduction from salary income under section 16(iii).

| State | Monthly deduction at the highest slab |
| Karnataka | INR 200 |
| Maharashtra | INR 200, and INR 300 in February |
| Telangana | INR 200 |
| Tamil Nadu | INR 1,250 per half year |
| Haryana | Not levied |

## 6. Labour Welfare Fund
Where the state levies a Labour Welfare Fund contribution, it is deducted at the notified rate and frequency and matched by the employer. The amounts are small and are shown separately in the payslip.

## 7. What to do on separation
- **Provident Fund**: transfer the balance to the new employer using Form 13 online through the UAN portal, or withdraw after 2 months of unemployment using Form 19 and Form 10C. Meridian does not need to act for an online transfer.
- **Gratuity**: no action is needed. Payment is initiated automatically from the last working day and is credited within 30 days.
- **ESI**: the coverage continues for the benefit period already earned.
