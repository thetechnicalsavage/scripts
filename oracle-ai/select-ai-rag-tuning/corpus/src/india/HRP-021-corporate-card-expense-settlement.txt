#BODY
## 1. Purpose
A corporate credit card removes the need for an employee to fund business expenses personally and wait for reimbursement. It is a company liability instrument issued in the employee's name, and it carries obligations that this policy sets out.

## 2. Eligibility and limits
| Grade band | Eligibility | Standard monthly limit |
| G1 to G4 | By exception, where the role requires travel of 8 or more days a month | INR 1,00,000 |
| G5 to G6 | On request, with function head approval | INR 2,50,000 |
| G7 to G8 | Automatic on appointment to the grade | INR 5,00,000 |
| G9 and above | Automatic on appointment to the grade | INR 10,00,000 |

A temporary limit increase for a specific trip is requested through an AskHR ticket in the Finance category at least 5 working days in advance, and is approved by the Finance Controller.

## 3. Permitted use
The card may be used only for expenses that are reimbursable under HRP-017, HRP-018 and HRP-019: air and rail tickets booked outside the travel desk, hotel settlement, ground transport, client entertainment approved in advance, and business supplies.

## 4. Prohibited use
- **Any personal expense, of any amount, for any reason.** There is no provision for "I will settle it later".
- Cash advances or ATM withdrawals, unless the card has been specifically enabled for a destination with limited card acceptance.
- Expenses for another employee who holds a card of their own.
- Purchases of capital assets, software licences or subscriptions. These go through the procurement process.
- Gifts to government officials or to anyone in a position to influence a Meridian business decision, which is separately prohibited by HRP-025.
- Alcohol, except within client entertainment expressly approved by the business unit head.

Personal use of the corporate card is treated as a financial integrity matter under HRP-028, not as a simple expense error.

## 4a. What to do if the card is used personally by mistake
Report it immediately through an AskHR ticket, before the statement cycle closes, and repay the amount to the company bank account within 7 days. A self-reported and promptly repaid error is treated as an administrative matter. An unreported personal charge discovered during the Finance audit is treated as misconduct.

## 5. Statement and settlement cycle
| Event | Timing |
| Statement cycle closes | 25th of each month |
| Statement available in MyMeridian | 27th of each month |
| Expense claim substantiating the charges due | Within **30 days** of the statement date |
| Company pays the card issuer | By the due date, regardless of whether the employee has claimed |

Because the company pays the issuer regardless, an unsubstantiated charge is an amount the company has paid out with no business record. This is why the settlement deadline is enforced.

## 6. Consequences of late settlement
| Occurrence in a rolling 12 months | Consequence |
| First late settlement | Reminder from Finance Shared Services and a note to the reporting manager |
| Second | Written notice; the reporting manager must acknowledge |
| Third | Card suspended for 90 days; the employee funds business expenses personally and claims reimbursement |
| Fourth | Card withdrawn permanently; unsubstantiated amounts recovered from payroll |

Amounts unsubstantiated for more than 60 days are recovered from the next payroll after a 7-day notice to the employee, without further approval.

## 7. Lost, stolen or compromised cards
Report the loss to the card issuer's 24-hour helpline immediately, and raise an AskHR ticket in the Finance category **within 24 hours**. The employee is not liable for fraudulent transactions reported within 24 hours of the loss. Delayed reporting may make the employee liable under the issuer's terms.

## 8. On separation
The card must be surrendered to the HR Business Partner on or before the last working day, and all outstanding charges must be substantiated. The clearance checklist in HRP-040 includes card surrender, and the relieving letter is not issued until Finance Shared Services confirms a nil position.
