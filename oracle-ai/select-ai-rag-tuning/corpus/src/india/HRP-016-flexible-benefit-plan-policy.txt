## 1. Purpose
The Flexible Benefit Plan (FBP) lets an employee allocate a defined part of fixed pay among components that carry a favourable tax treatment. This document explains the components, the limits, the declaration windows and the proof requirements.

The FBP is relevant only to employees who have chosen the **old tax regime**. Under the new tax regime introduced by section 115BAC, most of these exemptions are not available, and the FBP allocation has no tax effect. The regime choice is made at the start of the financial year.

## 2. FBP components and limits
| Component | Annual limit | Proof required | Exemption basis |
| House Rent Allowance | 50 per cent of Basic in Tier A cities, 40 per cent elsewhere | Rent receipts; landlord PAN if annual rent exceeds INR 1,00,000 | Section 10(13A) |
| Leave Travel Allowance | INR 60,000 per block of four calendar years | Travel tickets and boarding passes for domestic travel by the shortest route | Section 10(5) |
| Fuel and vehicle maintenance | INR 28,800 (INR 2,400 per month) | Fuel bills and a copy of the vehicle registration in the employee's name | Rule 3(2) |
| Telephone and internet | INR 18,000 (INR 1,500 per month) | Postpaid or broadband bills in the employee's name | Rule 3(7)(ix) |
| Meal card | INR 26,400 (INR 2,200 per month) | None; loaded onto a prepaid meal card | Rule 3(7)(iii) |
| Professional development | INR 25,000 | Course fee receipts, subject to HRP-038 | Rule 3(7)(vi) |
| Books and periodicals | INR 12,000 | Purchase receipts relevant to the role | Rule 3(7)(vi) |
| National Pension System, employer contribution | Up to 10 per cent of Basic | None; routed by payroll | Section 80CCD(2) |
| Car lease | As per the approved lease grid, G5 and above | Lease agreement through the company's leasing partner | Rule 3(2) |

Unclaimed FBP amounts are paid out as a fully taxable special allowance with the March payroll.

## 3. Declaration windows
| Window | Dates | What can be changed |
| Annual FBP declaration | 01 to 20 April | Tax regime choice, and the full FBP allocation |
| Mid-year revision | 01 to 15 October | FBP allocation only; the tax regime choice cannot be changed |
| Investment declaration | 01 to 20 April | Estimated deductions under Chapter VI-A, house property loss |
| Investment proof submission | 01 December to 15 January | Upload of actual proofs against the April declaration |

A declaration not submitted in the April window results in a default allocation of the entire FBP amount to a taxable special allowance, and tax computed under the **new regime**, which is the statutory default.

## 4. Investment proof submission
Proofs are uploaded in **MyMeridian > My Pay > Tax Declarations**. The deadline of **15 January** is firm, because payroll must compute the final tax liability and recover any shortfall across the February and March payrolls.

Where a proof is not submitted, the corresponding exemption is withdrawn and tax is recomputed. An employee who obtains a proof after the deadline can still claim the deduction directly in the income tax return; Meridian cannot reopen the payroll computation after the annual return is filed.

Commonly submitted proofs:
- Life insurance premium receipts, Public Provident Fund passbook, ELSS statements, tuition fee receipts, principal repayment certificate for a housing loan, under section 80C.
- Health insurance premium receipts for self and parents, under section 80D.
- Interest certificate for an education loan, under section 80E.
- A provisional interest certificate from the lender and the property possession letter, for a house property loss.
- Rent receipts and the landlord's PAN for House Rent Allowance.

## 5. Form 12BB and previous employer income
Every employee must file **Form 12BB** in MyMeridian by 15 January. Employees who joined Meridian during the financial year should also submit **Form 12B** from the previous employer along with the previous payslips and the tax deducted certificate, so that payroll computes the tax on total income for the year. Where this is not submitted, the employee may face a large liability at the time of filing the return, because both employers will have given the basic exemption separately.

## 6. Form 16
Form 16 for the financial year is issued by **15 June**. It is downloaded from MyMeridian > My Pay > Tax Documents. Part A is generated by the income tax department's TRACES system and Part B by payroll. A correction request must be raised as an AskHR ticket within 30 days of issue.
