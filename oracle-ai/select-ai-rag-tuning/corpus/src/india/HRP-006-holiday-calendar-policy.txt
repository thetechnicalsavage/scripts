## 1. Purpose
This document publishes the Meridian Systems India holiday calendar for the 2026 calendar year and explains how fixed and restricted holidays work. India has significant regional variation in public holidays, so the calendar is maintained per location.

## 2. Structure of the entitlement
Every employee receives **12 paid holidays** in a calendar year:

- **10 fixed holidays**, declared for the employee's base location and applicable to everyone at that location.
- **2 restricted holidays**, selected by the employee from the restricted list for the location.

Holidays falling on a Saturday or Sunday are not compensated with an alternative day, except where the location has fewer than 10 working-day holidays available, in which case HR Operations adds a bridge day.

## 6. Rules for restricted holidays
- A restricted holiday must be applied for in MyMeridian at least 3 working days in advance, like any other leave.
- The reporting manager may decline a restricted holiday only where there is a critical business commitment on that date, and must offer an alternative date from the list.
- Unused restricted holidays lapse on 31 December. They are not carried forward and are not encashed.
- Restricted holidays taken are not deducted from Earned, Casual or Sick Leave.

## 7. Working on a holiday
Where business need requires an employee to work on a fixed or restricted holiday, the work must be approved in advance by the reporting manager. The employee earns a Compensatory Off under HRP-001 section 6, or on-call and overtime payment under HRP-008 where applicable, but not both for the same period.

## 8. Employees working at a client site
An employee deployed at a client location may be required to follow the client's holiday calendar. Where the client calendar has fewer holidays than the Meridian calendar for that location, the difference is credited as additional Compensatory Off. Where it has more, the additional days are unpaid unless the client contract provides otherwise. The BU head confirms the applicable calendar to HR Operations at the start of each calendar year.
